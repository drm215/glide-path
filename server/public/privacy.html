Privacy policy

Your data in Glide Path.

Effective September 29, 2026

Without an account

Glide Path works without an account. Your courses, rounds, throws and bag are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

Location

The app reads your location only when you tap to save a tee or basket, log a throw, recenter the map, or find courses near you. It never tracks your location in the background. The website asks for your location only when you tap “Near me”, and uses it only for that search.

With an account

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address, the name you choose, and a securely hashed password (never the password itself).
  • The courses, finished rounds and bag you sync, including the GPS positions of tees, baskets and logged throws.

This data is used only to sync your devices and to show it back to you. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for tracking. There are no ads or third-party analytics.

What's public

Nothing you sync is public unless you choose it:

  • Published courses, including their details, notes, and tee and basket positions, are visible to everyone along with your chosen name.
  • Shared rounds can be seen by anyone with the link, along with your chosen name and the positions of that course's tees and baskets. Turning sharing off disables the link.

Services we rely on

  • Render hosts the Glide Path server and website, and Neon hosts its database.
  • DiscIt receives the disc names you search for in the Bag builder.
  • Apple Maps (in the app) and Esri (on the website) provide satellite imagery for the area you're viewing.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from the Account screen in the app, or from My rounds on this website. This permanently removes your account and everything synced to it, including published courses and shared round links. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
